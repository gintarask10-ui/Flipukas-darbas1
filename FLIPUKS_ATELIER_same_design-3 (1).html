About Flipukas Atelier

Made for people with a point of view.

Flipukas Atelier is a partner for people who want to create their own clothing brand but don’t want to manage every operational detail on their own. We help you move from idea to online store, then coordinate the agreed fulfillment steps when orders come in.

Your brand stays yours. Your voice, your audience, and your sales matter. Our role is to help make the process clearer and easier to manage.
